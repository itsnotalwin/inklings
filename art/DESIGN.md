# Art direction

Rich watercolor/sumi ink illustration with layered paper grain, cherry blossoms, old wood, delicate hatching, round pale Inky creatures and restrained red details. Warm tan/ivory paper, charcoal-black dry-brush outlines, dusty blush/burgundy and muted gray bodies. Keep organic pigment blooms and shadows; avoid vector-perfect geometry. The current title screen is a cropped concept image and needs future handmade cleanup of the art and button lettering. Actual rendered screenshots belong in the root and `src` folders, not as substitutes for artwork.
