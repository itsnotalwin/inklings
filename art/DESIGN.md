# Painted assets

`splash.jpg` is the actual original watercolor title image. Composition: aged rice paper, brush lettering, crimson circle and stamp, bell, cherry blossoms, clustered Inkies, terracotta bowl and three hand-painted title controls. Use the image itself rather than reconstructing the layout as generic UI. `nest-scene.jpg`, `pip.png`, `momo.png` and `paper.jpg` support the living nest.

Title art's native image ratio is 630:948. Responsive sizing may crop extreme background margins, but must never independently stretch characters or buttons. Local Chromium title screenshot at 390×844 was captured 2026-11-04; remote `SCREENSHOT.png` remains pending.
