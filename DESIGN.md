# INKLINGS — Watercolor visual target

The chosen illustration is the actual title artwork: painted wordmark, rose sun, bell, huddled Inkies, terracotta bowl, cherry blossom branches, and three painted menu pads. HTML button hotspots overlay the pads, not separate modern UI cards. On narrow phones the artwork fills the screen while preserving its native 630:948 proportions, with a soft continuation behind it.

Palette: aged paper, charcoal, dusty rose and moss. The nest retains feed, play, rest, pet, journal and settings interactions. No levels, deaths or time pressure.
