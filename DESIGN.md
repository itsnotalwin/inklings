# Root UI design and screenshot status

A full illustrated cover derived from the watercolor composition shown in the design reference, containing the title, gathered Inkies and the Play/Journal/Settings marks. The original painting is the primary UI; real transparent HTML buttons are placed over the painted controls for accessibility.

The living nest uses a crop of the watercolor scene and two character overlays, warm parchment (#e9ddc9), charcoal ink (#3c3430), dusty wine (#8d5052) and softly glowing lantern. Avoid generic interface panels and sharp vector gradients. Type should stay quiet and legible.

Viewports: portrait phones 320–430 px wide; preserve artwork's composition without horizontal overflow; large touch targets, safe areas, keyboard focus and reduced-motion preference.

Screenshot: actual local Chromium 390×844 render was generated during development, but not yet stored in the remote root as `SCREENSHOT.png` at this initial commit. Visual evidence is **incomplete** until screenshot is committed and checked against the published interface.
